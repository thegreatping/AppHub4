Cheat Sheet

What it is

A quarterly performance snapshot per property. It drives the Property Manager and Maintenance Supervisor bonuses, split into Leadership and Maintenance measures.

The three tabs

  • Scorecard — the pass/fail grid + Overall Score.
  • Fall Forecast — leasing vs. target (feeds the Bonus Calculation Form).
  • Compare Quarters — per-property deltas between two quarters.

Reading the grid

  • 1 = credit earned, 0 = not earned.
  • Overall Score = Leadership + Maintenance + RM Score.
  • An orange outline means a cell was manually overridden (hover for who & why).

Your inputs RM

Open a property's slideout to enter RM Score (0–5), Surveys, and Controllable NOI. They save instantly and the Overall Score updates.

Handy actions

  • Click a column header to sort or filter.
  • Click a row for the property slideout & notes.
  • Help & Criteria — definitions, bonus form, Paycom guide.
  • Feedback button — send us a note anytime.

Beta Testing / Demo Script

Walk through these in order. Note anything confusing or broken via the Feedback button.

  1. Open the Scorecard and confirm the login completes and the grid loads.
  2. Confirm the scope is right: RMs see their portfolio; PMs see their one property (read-only).
  3. Open Help & Criteria → Criteria. Search for a measure, then use Expand All / Collapse All.
  4. Click a column header to sort; click again to reverse. Open a column filter and narrow the list.
  5. Hover a measure header — confirm its definition appears as a tooltip.
  6. Click a property row to open the slideout; turn on “How was this scored?” and read an explanation.
  7. RM Enter an RM Score, mark Surveys and NOI, and save a note. Confirm the Overall Score updates.
  8. Open a drill-down (click a measure header) and review the source rows; try the “fails only” filter and an export.
  9. Switch to Fall Forecast; review Complete / New / Renewal vs. target and try Excel Export.
  10. Switch to Compare Quarters and pick two quarters (once more than one quarter exists).
  11. Use the Feedback button to send one real piece of feedback end-to-end.
