Schools ▼
Manage school markets and view aggregated market data by school. Each school card shows its market columns and linked comp properties.

How to use:
  • Search — Filter schools by name.
  • Show inactive — Toggle to include deactivated schools.
  • + Add School — Create a new school market entry.
Markets ▼
Create and manage market categories used to group schools for regional comparisons.

How to use:
  • Search — Filter markets by name.
  • + Add Market — Create a new market grouping.
Comps ▼
Manage competitor properties. Click any comp row to open the detail panel with all fields grouped by category.

How to use:
  • Search — Filter comp properties by name.
  • Show inactive — Toggle to include deactivated comps.
  • + Add Comp — Register a new competitor property.
  • Detail panel — Click a comp to edit rent, occupancy, amenities, and more.

Assign Comps ▼
Link comp properties to a parent property for a specific survey week. Only assigned comps appear in the Data Entry grid.

How to use:
  • Select a parent property and week from the left panel.
  • Drag or click comps in Available to move them to Assigned.
