About the Scorecard tab — click to expand

Purpose. Quarterly performance snapshot per property, broken into Leadership (measures the Property Manager owns) and Maintenance (measures the Maintenance Supervisor owns). Overall Score is the total across every measure plus the RM's subjective RM Score.

How to use it.

  • Rows are your properties. Each row totals to the Overall Score at the far left.
  • Green P = pass, red F = fail; the number in the RM Score column is the RM's subjective 0-5 rating. Cells with an orange border have been manually overridden — hover for who and why.
  • Click any row to open the property's slideout for detail, notes, and RM score entry.
  • The ribbon at the top shows how many communities you're seeing, how many RM Scores you've saved, and how many communities have comments.
  • Click any column header to sort. Use the filter row under the header names to narrow down. Use the search box for a quick free-text filter.

About the Fall Forecast tab — click to expand

Purpose. Snapshot of each property's current preleasing progress vs. target for the upcoming Fall academic year. Splits into Complete, New, and Renewal buckets so you can see whether target-vs-actual is off because renewals are slow, new leases are slow, or both.

How to use it.

  • Rows are your properties; columns pair actual (CY) vs. target with a variance column showing how far ahead or behind you are.
  • Positive variance = ahead of target (good). Negative = behind (needs attention).
  • Search box narrows to a specific property, entity, or RM.
  • The Bonus Calc Form Export button generates the standardized workbook for finance sign-off.

About the Compare Quarters tab — click to expand

Purpose. Pick two quarters and see per-property deltas on every measure plus the rollups (PRERM, Leadership Total, Maintenance Total, Overall). Great for RVPs reviewing trajectory and for prepping bonus-committee discussions.

How to use it.

  • Quarter A defaults to the quarter immediately before the current one; Quarter B defaults to the current quarter. Switch either to any historical quarter present in SCORECARD_CORE.
  • Green cells = improvement (B higher than A). Red cells = regression. Grey = no change or missing data.
  • Scoping follows your role: PMs see one property, RMs/RVPs see their portfolio, admins see all.
  • Read-only -- edits are made in the main Scorecard tab.
