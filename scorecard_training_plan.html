Leadership Scorecard - Training Plan

AppHub 4.0 - Leadership Scorecard. Scope: how the data flows, when it refreshes, who owns each source, and where we are automating next.

1. What the Scorecard does

Produces a quarterly leadership + maintenance score for every reportable property. Scores drive bonus eligibility for Regional Managers and Property-side teams. The grid shows 10 measures per property, rolled up to PRERM, LDRTOTAL, MSTOTAL, and an OVERALL score.

  • Admins see all properties and can edit anything.
  • Regional Managers / RVPs see their own portfolio and can edit the measures that are within their control (overrides, RM Score, notes).
  • Property Managers see their single property and are read-only.

4. Pipeline and refresh schedule

  • Notebook: NB_LEADERSHIP_SCORECARD_UPDATE (18 steps, ~45s runtime)
  • Runs: daily, inside the DAILY_UPDATE_PLUS_REFRESH pipeline (EID 20190101 in EXECUTION_PLAN)
  • Rebuilds SCORECARD_CORE from the sources above; honors override locks and manual RMSCORE entries (never overwrites them)
  • Status visible from the Scorecard admin tool: Notebook Runs button
